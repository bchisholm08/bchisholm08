I study how people hear in noisy places.

Research Professional in the Sensory Optimization & Neural Implant Coding Lab and the Center for Applied & Translational Sensory Science.

I run psychophysical and behavioral experiments on how listeners pick out sounds in noisy environments, and I handle the analysis of the data they produce. I am applying to PhD programs in hearing science and auditory neuroscience for Fall 2027.
